<p class="section-title">About</p>

<div class="about-box">
  <h2>Who we are</h2>
  <p>TMK Group is an independent mobile software publisher. We build practical, privacy-respecting apps for everyday life — covering health, productivity, finance, real estate, and cultural tools for communities worldwide.</p>
  <p>Our guiding principle: your data belongs to you. Most of our apps work fully offline and store everything on your device. Where a server is truly necessary, we say so clearly in the app's privacy policy and give you full control over your data.</p>
  <p>Every app's privacy policy is publicly available at <a href="https://privacy.tmkgroup.vn">privacy.tmkgroup.vn</a> and tracked in a public Git repository so any change is auditable.</p>
